# Rthquick

Rthquick is a Laravel 12 multivendor marketplace. Its two initial stores are Nous
Telos (Bengali heritage and handloom) and Bright (electrical products). The owner
approved Heaven as Bright's product brand; Bright also provides Heaven product
warranties. Phase 10 Phase 1 is in progress.
